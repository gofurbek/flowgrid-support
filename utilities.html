  • PriceLens: compare two packs using compatible units, multipack quantities and discounts.
  • ReturnPocket: save purchase deadlines, attach receipts and track return/refund follow-ups.

  • PackReady: create, duplicate, reset and share personal packing checklists.

  • BorrowNote: keep track of physical items lent or borrowed, with people, dates and attachments.
  • NextStep: follow your own timed routine steps with pause, resume and manual completion.

Common questions

How do I move my records?

Open Settings → Export complete backup. Keep the ZIP in a private place. On another device with the same app, use Settings → Import backup. Importing replaces current records after confirmation and validation. The backup includes referenced attachments; an active routine run is not exported.

Where is cloud sync?

These versions do not have accounts or cloud sync. Explicit backup and text sharing are available. Keep your own backup before uninstalling.

Why did a date reminder arrive late?

Android battery settings and notification permission affect reminders. These apps use approximate local date reminders, not guaranteed alarm-clock delivery.

Does ParkPin find GPS automatically?

No. Enter the garage or street note yourself. Coordinates are optional and entered manually. A maps app may need connectivity and does not provide indoor garage guidance through ParkPin.

Does NextStep speak or sound in the background?

No. Keep it open for immediate timer feedback. Elapsed time continues when you leave, but you complete each step yourself.

Are the calculations advice?

No. Prices, due dates, labels and schedules are based on what you enter. These tools do not provide financial, tax, legal, medical, payroll or food-safety advice.
