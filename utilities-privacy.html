Android utilities privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026 · version 1.0

This policy covers PriceLens, ReturnPocket, UseFirst, RenewalRadar, ShiftPocket, HomeRhythm, PackReady, ParkPin, BorrowNote and NextStep, published by Aqsaq Studio.

Records stay on your device

These releases work offline without an account or developer backend. Records you enter are saved in each app’s private storage. Depending on the app, this can include prices, purchase details, receipts, food label dates, subscriptions, working schedules, household tasks, checklists, parking notes, manually entered coordinates, items lent or borrowed, names you type and routine steps. The apps do not send these records to Aqsaq Studio.

No advertisements or tracking

Version 1.0 contains no advertising, analytics, attribution or billing SDK. There are no in-app purchases. The apps do not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or broad shared-storage permissions. ParkPin does not capture GPS or track your movements; its optional coordinates are entered by you.

Files, sharing and external apps

ReturnPocket, BorrowNote and ParkPin can import a selected JPEG, PNG or PDF using Android’s file picker. A copy is kept privately by that app. You choose the file; the app does not scan your device. When you open an attachment, the viewer you select receives temporary read access to that file.

Sharing a record or checklist, exporting a backup, or opening coordinates in a maps app happens only when you choose that action. Your chosen destination or other app receives the selected content and has its own data practices. Choose private destinations for receipts, schedules, names and coordinates.

Optional reminders

ReturnPocket, UseFirst, RenewalRadar, HomeRhythm and BorrowNote can ask for Android notification permission when you enable a date reminder. Reminders are scheduled locally; they are not push messages from a server. Denying permission does not prevent keeping your records. Android may delay reminders because of device or battery settings.

Backup and deletion

Automatic Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for these releases. You can export a ZIP backup from Settings and explicitly import it later. A ZIP backup includes records and referenced attachment files; it is not independently encrypted by the app. Protect it using your chosen storage destination.

Delete individual records where offered, clear an app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall it to remove its local records. Unreferenced attachment copies are removed after saved records no longer use them. Exported backups and copies you shared are outside the app and must be removed separately from their destinations. Export a backup before uninstalling or replacing your phone if you want to retain your data.

Support

If you email aqsaq.soft@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and retain it only as needed for that request and applicable obligations. Do not email sensitive receipts, identity documents or other confidential information.

Google Play and this website

Google Play and Android may process installation, security or diagnostic information under their own policies. This website’s hosting provider may process routine connection data such as IP addresses and request details to deliver these pages. Website access is separate from the apps’ offline operation.

Changes

We will update this policy if a release’s data practices change. Any later advertising or connected feature will require a separate app update and corresponding disclosures.
