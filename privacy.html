Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

Flowgrid is an offline circuit puzzle game published by Aqsaq Studio. This policy describes the ad-free Android release.

Game data stays on your device

Your puzzle progress, settings, undo history, best scores, and daily streaks are stored locally on your device. Flowgrid has no user accounts, developer-operated backend, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK. The game does not send this information to Aqsaq Studio.

Information and permissions

Flowgrid does not request your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, photos, or payment details. It does not display advertisements, sell personal data, or use advertising identifiers. The Android web container may retain internet permission, but the game does not require a connection for play.

Your choices

You can change sound, haptics, language, and motion settings in the game. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes local progress. Depending on your device settings, your operating system may back up or restore app data under its own policies. If you choose to share a puzzle result, your device sends the result to the app or recipient you select.

Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request and retain it only as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information in support messages.

Policy and support website

The hosting provider for this policy or the support page may process routine connection data, such as IP address and request information, to deliver and protect the website. This is separate from the offline game.

Audience

Flowgrid is a general-audience logic game and is not specifically directed to children under 13.

Changes

If the game or its data practices change, this policy and its effective date will be updated.
