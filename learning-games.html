Learning games support

  • Math Garden: Build confidence with addition, subtraction and multiplication. Choose a practice level, answer a question, and see the correct answer and explanation immediately.
  • Color Splash: Recognize eight colors through clear color swatches and multiple-choice answers. Practice at your own pace with immediate feedback.
  • Shape Explorer: Explore circles, triangles, squares, rectangles, pentagons and hexagons. Look at a shape, choose its name, and see the answer immediately.
  • Animal Detective: Read a short clue and choose the animal it describes. Explore a small collection of animal riddles with immediate answers and explanations.
  • Opposite World: Explore words with opposite meanings. Choose an answer to a short vocabulary question and see the correct answer immediately.
  • Clock Club: Read the hands of an analog clock and choose the time shown. Five practice levels progress from full hours to individual minutes.
  • Memory Spark: Study a short symbol sequence, hide it when you are ready, and choose the matching sequence. Five levels increase the sequence length from three to seven symbols.
  • Pattern Play: Look at a repeating symbol pattern and choose what comes next. Five levels increase pattern length from two to six symbols.
  • Junior Reasoning: Practice categories, order and simple reasoning with short multiple-choice puzzles. This is informal practice for enjoyment, not a standardized assessment or an IQ measurement.
  • IQ Test: Logic & Patterns: Practice number sequences, visual patterns, logic and word relationships with immediate feedback and explanations. No sign-up is required. This informal practice does not measure IQ.

IQ Test: Logic & Patterns

Choose a Quick (10 questions), Classic (20) or Extended (30) session. Select Mixed, Numbers, Patterns, Logic or Words. Tap an answer for immediate feedback and an explanation, then continue. Results show correct answers, accuracy, elapsed time and a category breakdown. Review your answers or try another test. Last results and best completed-session accuracy for each focus and session length stay on your device. This app does not provide a standardized or validated IQ score.

Other learning games: how to play

Choose a session of 10, 25, 50 or 100 questions, or endless practice. Choose one of five levels and tap Let's play. Tap an answer to see feedback, then tap Next question. End round finishes a session early. In Memory Spark, use the hide-symbols button before choosing an answer.

Other learning games: saved results

Last-round results and best accuracy per level remain on your device. They are not synchronized. Clear app storage or uninstall to reset them. Short and long rounds share the best-accuracy statistic.

Question variety and accessibility

Long sessions repeat and remix small question banks. Some levels share the same full question bank. Colors, shapes and clocks rely on visual information and do not provide an equivalent nonvisual activity. Young players may need reading help.
