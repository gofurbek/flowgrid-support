How to play

Tap a tile to turn it clockwise. Connect every tile to the glowing core with no loose ends. Undo takes back a move. Restart resets the current puzzle.

Hints

Hints are free and correctly orient one unsolved tile. There are no advertisements or in-app purchases.

Daily pulse

The daily puzzle changes at midnight UTC. Daily streaks and personal records are saved on your device.

Saved progress

Your game saves automatically. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes local progress. Progress is not synchronized between devices.
