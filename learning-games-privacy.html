Learning games privacy policy

Effective 4 November 2026

This policy covers the ad-free Android releases of Math Garden, Color Splash, Shape Explorer, Animal Detective, Opposite World, Clock Club, Memory Spark, Pattern Play, Junior Reasoning, IQ Test: Logic & Patterns.

Offline play and local records

The games work offline without an account. Round results and best practice accuracy are saved in private app storage on your device. The games do not send these records to Aqsaq Studio or operate a developer backend.

No advertising, tracking or sensitive permissions

These releases contain no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, in-app purchases or third-party runtime libraries. They do not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or shared-storage permissions. They do not collect or share personal information through the game.

Children and families

Several of these games are designed for children's learning. The same offline data practices apply to children and adults. There is no chat, user-generated content, advertising or account registration. Some activities require reading support or vision; they are not educational or psychological assessments.

Deleting local records

Clear the game's app storage in Android settings or uninstall the game to remove its saved results. Android backup is disabled for these releases.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use that information to answer your request and retain it only as needed for the request and applicable obligations. A parent or guardian should contact support on behalf of a child. Do not send sensitive information.

This website

The website hosting provider may process routine connection data, such as IP addresses and request details, to deliver and protect these pages. Website access is separate from offline gameplay.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the games' data practices change.
